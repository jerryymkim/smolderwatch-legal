Smolderwatch — Privacy Policy

App: Smolderwatch (com.jerrykim.smolderwatch)  ·  Developer: Kaiomene

Effective date: June 28, 2026  ·  Contact: jerryymkim@gmail.com

Smolderwatch ("the game", "we", "us") is a single-player idle role-playing game. This policy explains what information the game handles, why, and the choices you have. We keep data collection to the minimum needed to show optional ads, sync your saved game, and process purchases.

Data deletion requests

To request deletion of your data, email jerryymkim@gmail.com with the subject line "Smolderwatch data deletion". We respond to deletion requests within 30 days.

What gets deleted: your cloud-saved game progress (zones, currencies, upgrades, settings) stored via Google Play Games Saved Games. You can also delete this yourself at any time through Google Play Games ("Delete Play Games data").

What is kept: nothing that identifies you. The optional anonymous analytics use a random install ID that is not linked to your identity, so there is no personal record to delete. Your local save lives only on your device and is removed when you uninstall the app. Retention of advertising and identifier data handled by Google AdMob is governed by Google.

What we collect

The game itself does not ask you for your name, email address, or any personal profile. The information involved is collected and processed by the third-party Google services the game uses, plus an optional first-party analytics feature you control.

1. Advertising — Google AdMob

When you choose to watch a rewarded ad, Google AdMob may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising identifier (Advertising ID),
  • device information (model, operating-system version, language, coarse region),
  • ad-interaction data (which ads were shown and completed).

Google uses this to serve and measure ads. Ads are optional — they load only when you tap a "watch ad" button for a bonus. See Google's Privacy Policy and how AdMob handles data.

2. Cloud save — Google Play Games Services

If you sign in to Google Play Games, the game stores your saved progress in your Google account (Play Games "Saved Games" / Snapshots) so you can restore it on another device. This uses your Play Games player ID and your saved-game data — which is only your in-game progress (zones, currencies, upgrades, settings). Cloud save is optional; the game works fully offline with a local save if you don't sign in.

3. Purchases — Google Play Billing

In-app purchases are processed entirely by Google Play. The game receives only a confirmation that a purchase succeeded (so it can grant the item) — it never sees or stores your payment-card details.

4. Anonymous analytics (on by default — opt out anytime)

The game includes a built-in analytics feature that is on by default. While "Send anonymous analytics" is enabled in Options, the game sends the following to a first-party endpoint (a private Google Sheet operated by the developer):

  • A random install ID — a 32-character hex string generated by the game on first run. It is not linked to your Google account, device advertising ID, name, email, or any other identifier. It exists only to group events from the same install in the data.
  • Gameplay events: session start, zone reached (new personal-best zones only), prestige, hero death, and special-event triggers. No tap counts, no kill counts. No location, no device model, no OS version.

Opting out: toggle "Send anonymous analytics" off in Options at any time. Future events stop immediately. The random install ID is not linkable to your identity, so there is nothing to delete — the data is anonymous numbers.

This feature is entirely the developer's own code — it does not use any third-party analytics SDK. We do not use any other separate analytics or tracking SDK, and we do not sell your personal information.

How the information is used

  • To show the rewarded ads you choose to watch, and keep them relevant (AdMob).
  • To back up and restore your game progress across devices (Play Games).
  • To deliver the items you purchase (Play Billing).

Your choices and consent

  • Ads are opt-in per view — you only ever see one when you tap to watch.
  • EEA / UK (GDPR): before any personalized ads load, the game shows Google's consent dialog (UMP). You can change or withdraw consent at any time from Settings → Privacy → "Ad privacy & consent" inside the game.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete it in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads), which limits ad personalization.
  • Cloud save: decline the Google Play Games sign-in to keep all of your data local to your device.

Retention and deletion

  • Your local save lives on your device; uninstalling the app removes it.
  • Cloud saves live in your Google account — you can delete them through Google Play Games ("Delete Play Games data") or by emailing the contact address above.
  • Retention of advertising and identifier data is governed by Google.

See the Data deletion requests section above for step-by-step instructions.

Children

Smolderwatch is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your region). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Effective date" above will change, and material updates will be noted on the store listing.
